Intervention Framework

The ward-specific suggestions in this prototype are generated by transparent rules using LST, NDVI, NDBI, and NDWI. After model training, the same panel can show model-predicted LST/UHI and feature-based explanations.

Individual / Household

  • Use light-coloured, high-reflectance roof coatings where feasible.
  • Plant native shade trees and shrubs in compounds.
  • Add shade nets, pergolas, or vertical greenery to reduce direct solar gain.
  • Improve roof insulation and ventilation.

Community / RWA / Campus

  • Create pocket parks and shaded community spaces.
  • Increase roadside tree canopy and shade over parking areas.
  • Use permeable or lighter paving for pathways and open grounds.
  • Develop rooftop gardens on suitable community buildings.

Business / Institution

  • Adopt cool roofs and high-albedo building materials.
  • Create green buffers around parking and paved areas.
  • Design shaded walkways and courtyards.
  • Reduce unshaded asphalt and add rainwater-sensitive landscaping.

Policy / Urban Planning

  • Set tree-canopy and minimum-green-cover targets by ward.
  • Offer cool-roof and rooftop-garden incentives.
  • Protect mature trees and lake/wetland buffers.
  • Include shade, ventilation, and heat-resilience standards in urban design.

Methodology

  1. Downloaded Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level-2 imagery acquired on 25 April 2026.
  2. Used corrected BBMP 2022 ward boundaries for Bengaluru.
  3. Masked fill, cloud, cirrus, dilated cloud, cloud-shadow, and snow pixels through QA_PIXEL.
  4. Calculated LST from ST_B10, NDVI from B5 and B4, NDBI from B6 and B5, and NDWI from B3 and B5.
  5. Calculated ward-level mean values for 243 wards and evaluated relationships among the indicators.
